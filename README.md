# New Reno

The full city, not a sample. This repository is the current game.

Walk the streets. The map does not teleport: a route is a path along the roads, and the address book keeps corners you save plus how people feel about you. Time runs one Reno second per real second, with Hold, 5×, 15×, and 60×. It stops while a fight, a sighting, a conversation, or a body search is waiting on you.

Jobs are shifts. A counter is hours and then you are off. A family keeps you on call. The Strip stays a place people spend money. There is no police; the families keep their own code. Hunger and thirst matter in the desert and the unpowered edge, not on a working block.

`src/` is the game. `public/reno` is the art.
